Who We Are

Mission, Vision & Goal

Mission

To contribute to the advancement of technological, economic and social development of young people, through self-reliance, to help build a better world where people are self-fulfilled as individuals and groups; and play a constructive role in nation building.

Vision

“Provision of an enabling environment for the realization, respect, and protection of human rights and advancement of human development among youths to enable them to become active contributors in societal development.”

Goal

To contribute in improving the standard of living of South Sudanese people through mobilization, research, development and strengthening of local institutions across the country.

What We Do

Thematic Programmes

RDCOSS addresses South Sudan's most pressing challenges through a holistic portfolio of research, policy, and community‑based programmes.

National Economic Policy

Lobbying for the creation of a flexible National Economic policy to foster sustainable growth and opportunity.

National Health Policy

Advocating for an improved, comprehensive National Health policy that serves all South Sudanese.

Access to Communication

Lobbying for universal access to communication technologies and information for all citizens.

National & International Networking

Building partnerships with national and international bodies to amplify impact and share knowledge.

Research on Discrimination & Corruption

Researching all forms of discrimination, corruption, and tribal conflicts, and disseminating findings for public awareness.

Self-Help & Community Empowerment

Disseminating ideals and strategies of self-help to communities across South Sudan and empowering local institutions.

National Education Policy

Advocating for the strengthening of the National Education policy to ensure quality learning for all.

Emergency Response & Preparedness

Building capacity for rapid, effective response to natural and man‑made disasters.

Legal Aid & Education

Providing free legal aid and education to protect the rights of the vulnerable and marginalized.

Peace & Democracy

Disseminating peace, democracy, and good governance at all levels of society.

Gender Equality

Promoting gender equality and ensuring women's full participation in decision‑making processes.

Agricultural Extension

Training local farmers and promoting agribusiness to enhance food security and rural livelihoods.

Active Agriculture & Empowerment

Girl Motivation Project

2021

Research and Development Center Organization – South Sudan (RDCOSS)

Juba / Yirol, Lakes State 20 Vulnerable Girls Duration: 2021 Budget: SSP 6,854,600

Executive Summary

Child marriage remains a major challenge in South Sudan, affecting 60% of young girls nationally. This project aims to train, equip, and empower 20 young vulnerable girls affected by child early marriage through groundnut growing and production. By providing skills, tools, and a revolving fund, the project enables these girls to enhance their incomes, achieve economic independence, and break the cycle of poverty and exploitation.

Key Objectives
  • ✓ Equip 20 girls with sustainable groundnut growing skills
  • ✓ Strengthen capacity for value addition & quality production
  • ✓ Achieve economic empowerment to resist exploitation
Expected Outcomes
  • ✓ Girls gain employment & self-reliance
  • ✓ Confidence to confront child early marriage
  • ✓ Saving culture initiated & adopted
Sustainability Plan

Groundnuts have a ready market in South Sudan. The project utilizes locally available raw materials and equipment, with a revolving fund ensuring long-term sustainability. Financial returns from groundnut sales are reinvested, and a saving culture is established among beneficiaries.

Ongoing Recovery & Resilience

Accelerating Recovery & Transition Towards Self-Sufficiency

24 Months

Research and Development Center Organization – South Sudan (RDCOSS)

Yirol West & Lainya 60 Beneficiaries 24 Months Budget: USD 58,680

Summary

Lakes and Central Equatoria States are under severe strain from communal violence, returnee influx, and deep poverty. This 24-month intervention targets 60 vulnerable beneficiaries — girls affected by early marriage, returnees, IDPs, orphans, and people with disabilities — combining life-saving support (food, NFIs, health, protection) with livelihood and vocational skilling to move from crisis response to durable economic independence.

Key Objectives
  • ✓ Mitigate protection risks through advocacy & awareness
  • ✓ Increase capacity to withstand disaster impacts
  • ✓ Strengthen resilience through diversified income activities
  • ✓ Alleviate suffering through life-saving assistance
Key Activities
  • ✓ Agricultural inputs & vocational training
  • ✓ Sewing & tailoring, baking materials
  • ✓ Village Savings & Loan Associations (VSLAs)
  • ✓ Community sensitization & referral pathways

60

Direct Beneficiaries

24

Months Duration

$58,680

Total Budget

Sustainability

Village Savings and Loan Associations provide independent savings mechanisms. Vocational skills and agricultural assets create durable income bases. Community sensitization and referral pathways build local capacity. Programme continuity is protected with staff retained for six months post-project.

Our Approach

Strategy & Philosophy

Sustainable development through mobilization, training, empowerment, involvement, and partnership.

1. Mobilize

Engage communities and stakeholders to identify needs and opportunities for development.

2. Train

Build capacity through skills development, knowledge transfer, and expert-led training.

3. Empower

Enable communities to take ownership of their development through self-reliance and agency.

4. Partner

Collaborate with national and international bodies for sustainable, scalable impact.

“Sustainable development is a dynamic process posing various needs at different phases of time. These can be dealt with continuous experts' interventions based on designed frameworks that incorporate the requirements projected by the center and with the help of the support agencies by strategizing for public and private partnership.”

— RDCOSS Philosophy
